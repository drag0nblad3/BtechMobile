# BattleMech damage diagram

The live Sheet panel shows a generic front-facing biped schematic or a top-down four-legged schematic. Each location displays current/original armor (A) and internal structure (S). The three rear torso armor facings are shown separately; their structure is shared with the front.

Colors: green intact, yellow worn, orange at half armor or less, red exposed or internal damage, gray crossed-out destroyed, blue flooded. Actual numeric pip counts remain visible. Partial damage also affects the color when hardened armor or reinforced structure carries an odd damage point. This is a view of the existing record sheet, not a new damage calculation or hit table.
