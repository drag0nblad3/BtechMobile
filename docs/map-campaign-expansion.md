# Campaign expansion: rules boundary

Reference: Campaign Operations, fourth printing, map-based campaigns pp. 146–159.
- GM chooses scale, objectives and force size (pp. 146–151). Region dimensions, victory share, day cap and enemy skill are scenario parameters, not universal rules.
- One formation order per strategic turn, simultaneous resolution (p. 152). Reachable-zone overlays query MapOrders rather than introducing a second movement rule.
- Post-battle formation changes are free before formation orders (p. 156). HQ swaps require grounded co-located friendly lances and no pending encounters. No free healing or economic changes.
- Key location benefits (pp. 157–158): existing depot, city, fortress, factory and port mechanisms remain unchanged. Roads are cartographic only; no speculative supply-line penalties.
- Optional double-blind play (p. 159) differs from the app's simplified visibility radius. Last-known contacts only use observed zones and explicitly show stale information.
- Linked map campaigns are a GM-style optional framework (Narrative Campaigns, p. 145; Map-Based Campaigns, p. 146). Region advancement retains company personnel, condition, losses and history, regenerates the opponent and does not heal for free.
- Persistent battlefield seed reproduces base terrain, not destruction from earlier battles. Tactical dice remain separate.
- No claim of exhaustive Campaign Operations implementation. Full logistics, force transportation between worlds and historical roster restrictions require separate verified rules work.
