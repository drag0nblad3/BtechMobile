# Opening Warchest budget

New Chaos Campaign companies retain the original 1,000 WP. Contract costs remain 75% of the full reward pool, including offered options, and later debt rules are unchanged.

For any company that has not completed a battle, has no active contract or debt history, and has only unaffordable offers, the board replaces its cheapest offer with a valid lower-cost contract using two objectives and two options, if affordable. It keeps the mission and enemy BV, generates a new briefing, and does not credit the player with extra funds. Existing affordable boards are preserved. The initial selection favors an affordable offer. The contract list and footer show projected WP remaining or debt before acceptance.

With the shipped data, the minimum legal generated track costs 488 WP, so the player can still go into debt after discretionary spending below that threshold, or by choosing a more expensive contract.
