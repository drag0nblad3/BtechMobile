# Opening Warchest budget

Starting funds are a GM-selected setup value. New Chaos Campaign companies begin with 1,600 WP rather than 1,000. The maximum generated contract costs 1,425 WP under the existing 75% price rule; even the full 100 WP monthly support conversion leaves 75 WP after accepting that track.

For existing companies that have not completed a battle, have no active contract or debt history, and have only unaffordable offers, the board may replace its cheapest offer with a valid lower-cost contract using two objectives and two options. It keeps the mission and enemy BV, generates a new briefing, and does not credit the player with extra funds. This does not guarantee affordability after discretionary spending below the minimum legal contract cost (488 WP with current data). Later contracts, payouts and the one-contract debt rule are unchanged. The contract list and footer show projected WP remaining or debt before acceptance.
