# Unit favorites

Tap the hollow star beside a design to bookmark it; tap the filled star to remove it. Bookmarks are exact catalog design IDs (including individual OmniMech configurations, vehicles, battle armor and support units). They are device-local and stored in `user://unit_favorites.cfg`, separate from saved campaigns and battles.

Favorites appear first in the force catalog, both displayed force rosters, technical readout results, equipment usage lists, campaign market and company roster. Existing filters still apply, and the existing ordering is preserved within the favorite and nonfavorite sections. For grouped configurations, a group with a matching favorite appears first and defaults to its first favorite unless the player has explicitly cycled to another configuration.

Display order does not affect deployed force order, initiative, rules, actual campaign rosters, shop inventory, or removal indices. Formation templates and historical scenarios retain their prescribed composition; battle cycling remains tactical.
