# Historical Operations

Three playable company-scale adaptations, not transcriptions of published tracks. Open **Map Campaign → Historical Operations** when no map campaign is active. Both sides have persistent starting rosters; the usual map campaign orders, battles, damage, repairs, ownership and victory rules decide the outcome. No canonical deaths, wins or retreats are scripted. Historical comparison is displayed after the result.

## Evidence
- Benet III (7 June 3049): Sword and Dragon, pp. 32–37 and 80; roster transcriptions at [McKinnon's Company](https://www.sarna.net/wiki/McKinnon%27s_Company) and [Sorenson's Sabres](https://www.sarna.net/wiki/Sorenson%27s_Sabres). Both companies use their documented three-lance personnel assignments. Seven unverified/unavailable variants have clearly disclosed period substitutes. The original book also contains individual modified machines; the game uses standard catalog variants unless specifically sourced. No claim of exact individual loadouts.
- Galtor (3025): [Black Widow Company](https://www.sarna.net/wiki/Black_Widow_Company), [21st Illician Rangers](https://www.sarna.net/wiki/21st_Illician_Rangers), [Levon Grant](https://www.sarna.net/wiki/Levon_Grant), citing Tales of the Black Widow Company, pp. 18–19. Grant's lance has documented chassis only; standard variants and additional two opposing lances are reconstructed.
- Misery (23 April 3028): [official Historical Turning Points preview](https://battletech.com/wp-content/uploads/10%20Previews/CAT35621_HTP%20Misery_Preview.pdf), pp. 5–7 and opening Hamar Valley track. Named Black Widow roster as above; three opposing lances are illustrative selections of catalog designs appearing in the campaign's assignment tables, not a claim about exact participants.

## Boundaries
All strategic maps are original 5×5 schematic sectors, not published maps or true geographic scale; orientation, deployments, VP, skills (4/5) and day count are adaptation choices. Historical factions are descriptive metadata, not generic MUL force replacement: explicit roster evidence takes priority over broad availability. Standard repair and retreat rules are unchanged, no factory reinforcements, and the capture objective is a game framework, not the original scenario victory rules. Misery uses existing cold and snowfall flags, not unsupported tactical weather rules. Source books may contain earlier/later variants, individual pilot abilities, damage and special rules not reproduced. No proprietary maps, record sheets or prose copied.

Exact missing variants and disputed transcriptions remain in the data as explicit recorded_variant fields, shown before launch.
