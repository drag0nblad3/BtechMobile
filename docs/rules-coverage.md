# Rules coverage (Total Warfare)

Status: ✅ done + tested · 🟡 partial · ⬜ not started. Keep one line per rule.

## Turn sequence
- ✅ Initiative (p. 39): 2d6 per side, ties reroll with the same modifiers, loser moves first; totals may pass 2 or 12.
- ✅ Initiative Die (optional, `InitiativeDie`, toggled on the start screen): the loser gets +1 per loss in a row (max +6) until it wins.
- ✅ Unit alternation with unequal numbers (`UnequalNumbers`, p. 41): the bigger side spreads its 'Mechs over the smaller side's turns, extra ones first; recounted each phase.
- ✅ Phases: Initiative → Movement → Weapon Attack → Physical Attack → Heat → End.
- ✅ Attacks declared unit by unit in initiative order, resolved together at phase end
  (a 'Mech destroyed this phase still fires).
- ✅ Game ends when one side (or none) has 'Mechs left.

## Ground movement ('Mechs)
- ✅ Walking / running MP (run = walk × 1.5 rounded up); -1 walk MP per leg actuator hit (hip, upper, lower, foot), 1 with a leg gone, 0 with both.
- ✅ Facing changes: 1 MP per hexside.
- ✅ Terrain costs: clear/pavement +0, rough +1, light woods +1, heavy woods +2.
- ✅ Roads (`Roads`, p. 53): paved hex to paved hex costs the base 1 MP and a level change between them 1 MP less (minimum 0) — `TODO(rules)`: roads through prohibited terrain (our maps pave whole hexes); bridge decks count once bridges land.
- ✅ Level change: 1 MP per level, max 2 levels per hex.
- ✅ Water (p. 47): depth 1 +1, depth 2+ +2, plus the level change to the bottom; running in is allowed but costs a piloting roll.
- ✅ Hip hit or destroyed leg: 1 level per hex forward, none backward.
- ✅ Stacking: pass through friends, not enemies; one 'Mech per hex at end of movement.
- ✅ Backward walking (not running), same costs, at most 1 level change; any backward level change costs a piloting roll, and a failure falls (in the lower hex).
- ✅ Jumping (p. 50): 1 MP per hex, any landing facing, ignores terrain costs; no hex higher than start level + jump MP (woods don't count, buildings do); from water the start is the bottom, depth 2+ can't jump, leg jets don't fire in depth 1; landing in water costs a roll; heat 1/hex (min 3); +3 own, +1 extra to be hit; 'Mech-scale buildings are landed on, on the roof.
- ✅ Standing up: 2 MP plus a piloting roll (-1 helps it); failing means falling again, then it tries again while the planned move leaves 2 spare MP (p. 51); standing frees the facing. Impossible with a
  destroyed gyro, both legs gone, or one leg and both arms gone (p. 51).
- ✅ Dropping prone (`ProneMoves`, p. 51): in place, or at the end of a walk/run with 1 MP to spare (the Prone button uses the previewed move); not after jumping.
- 🟡 Prone 'Mech that can't stand (destroyed gyro, both legs gone) only turns in its hex, 1 MP a hexside; a destroyed gyro allows one hexside a turn (p. 46) — `TODO(rules)`: a prone 'Mech that could stand can't choose to turn without standing.
- ✅ Minimum movement (p. 49): running, one hex straight ahead for all MP, even if it costs more.
- ✅ Movement piloting rolls (`MovePsr`): rolled the moment the hex is entered for running into water, rubble, backward level changes and landing in water (a failure ends the move with a fall); rolled after the move for running 1+ hex with a gyro/hip hit or leg gone, and jumping with gyro, leg or leg actuator damage.
- ⬜ Skidding: not in the Core Rulebook; `Skid` is kept for an optional rule but never used.
- ⬜ Lateral shift (optional rule).

## Line of sight
- ✅ LOS levels (p. 57): ground (water bottom) + 2 standing / 1 prone. A hex intervenes if its top (hill; woods +2; building roof) is at least both LOS levels, or at least the attacker's and next to it, or at least the target's and next to it. Intervening hills and buildings block; woods add 1/2 points, 3 block.
- ✅ Divided LOS (along a hexside): the target's side chooses; the game takes the side worse for the attacker.
- ✅ Partial cover from terrain (p. 66): a standing target next to a hex one level above its ground, on the line, gets +1 and leg hits strike the cover (a building takes the damage), unless the attacker's LOS level is higher; indirect fire ignores it; civilian buildings and woods give none.
- ✅ Leg-mounted weapons can't fire through a hill or building hex that gives the firing 'Mech partial cover (`LegFire`, p. 61); water has its own rules.
- 🟡 Water combat (`WaterCombat`, pp. 62, 66-67, 127): underwater = depth 2+ or prone in depth 1, wading = standing in depth 1. No shots across the water line; only energy weapons fire from a submerged location (a wading 'Mech's leg weapons only at targets in water, its other weapons not at underwater targets); shots from underwater, or from leg weapons at a wading target, hit legs on the Kick table with no partial cover; underwater shots need a line of water hexes; energy weapons fire one range bracket longer, long range out. Water never blocks line of sight — `TODO(rules)`: torpedoes.
- ✅ Hull breaches (`HullBreach`, p. 127): damage to a location under water rolls 2D6, 2-4 floods it; a bare location (no armor, front or rear for a torso) under water floods at once, now or after moving or falling. A flooded location stops working as if destroyed, without explosions or crits (later crits there do nothing except ammo); a flooded leg counts as destroyed, a flooded side torso floods its arm; it stays flooded out of the water. The record sheet marks it.

## Weapon attacks
- ✅ To-hit: gunnery + attacker movement (walk +1, run +2) + target movement
  (3-4 +1, 5-6 +2, 7-9 +3, 10-17 +4, 18-24 +5, 25+ +6) + range (+0/+2/+4) + minimum range.
- ✅ Woods: intervening +1 light / +2 heavy, target hex the same.
- ✅ Heat to-hit (+1 at 8, +2 at 13, +3 at 17, +4 at 24); sensor hits +2 each; prone attacker +2.
- ✅ Firing while prone (`ProneFire`, p. 67): needs an arm; the propping arm (picked automatically: the one with less firepower) and leg weapons can't fire.
- ✅ Prone target (-2 adjacent, +1 otherwise); immobile target -4 (shut down, unconscious or 0 walking MP); secondary target +1 in any arc, and the primary must be in the forward arc if any target is (p. 64).
- ✅ Partial cover from depth 1 water: +1, leg hits absorbed.
- ✅ Firing arcs (p. 60): forward 120°, sides 60°, rear 120°; arms add their side; rear mounts fire rear. Hit zones (p. 73) keep the hexside shape: front is 3 hexsides.
- ✅ Torso twist one hexside in the weapon phase; leg weapons keep the legs' facing; punches use the torso.
- ✅ Flipping arms (`ArmFlip`, p. 62): a design with neither lower arm nor hand actuators in both arms may flip them in the weapon phase instead of twisting ("Flip arms" button); its arm weapons (and an arm AMS) then cover only the rear arc, it can't punch or swing an arm weapon, not while prone, and the arms swing back at the end of the turn. The computer flips when that gives the best volley.
- ✅ Target number 2 or less always hits (p. 72); target > 12 can't be attempted.
- ✅ Cluster hits table, all 13 columns (p. 74), checked against the book; LRMs in 5-point groups, SRMs per missile.
- ✅ Ammo used per shot; no ammo, no shot.
- ✅ Flamers (pp. 183-184): a hit deals damage and the same amount of heat to the target (ER flamer: only 1 heat, `heat_points`); outside heat is capped at 15 per turn.
- ✅ Sturdy autocannons (`AutocannonCrit`, p. 183): the first critical hit on any autocannon (AC, Ultra, LB-X, rotary) marks the slot but the gun still fires; a second hit on another of its slots disables it; a one-slot AC needs its slot hit twice.
- 🟡 Indirect LRM fire (`IndirectFire`, pp. 62, 70): a friendly 'Mech with line of sight spots one target per turn; +1 indirect, + spotter's movement, +1 if the spotter also attacks (and +1 to the spotter's own attacks); terrain counted from the spotter; a 'Mech that declared a charge or DFA can't spot.
- ✅ Aimed shots (`AimedShot`, p. 70): against an immobile target, a non-cluster, non-indirect shot (no Ultra double fire, no LB-X cluster rounds) may aim at a location; -4 kept, but a head shot drops it and takes +3; on a hit 1D6 4+ lands there, else the location is rolled; with partial cover legs can't be chosen, the cover modifier is dropped and leg rolls are rerolled. The weapon panel shows an "Aim at" button for immobile targets (`AimPicker`); the computer aims too (`BotAim`).

## Damage
- ✅ Hit location tables (front/rear, left, right), punch and kick tables (pp. 74, 81): checked against the book; the front punch table had arms and side torsos swapped, now fixed.
- ✅ Armor → structure → transfer inward; rear armor on torsos.
- ✅ Side torso destroyed takes its arm; head or CT destroyed destroys the 'Mech.
- ✅ Ammo explosion (pp. 95-96): remaining shots × damage, capped at 20, into structure, transferring structure-to-structure; over the cap the surviving last location loses its armor (rear for a torso); MechWarrior takes 1 hit.
- ✅ Explosive components (`ComponentExplosion`, pp. 96, 184): a Gauss rifle explodes on its first critical hit for 2 damage per slot (14), handled like an ammo explosion (20 cap, CASE, pilot hit); later hits don't explode it again; Gauss slugs never explode — `TODO(rules)`: always treated as activated (p. 180).

- ✅ Advanced ballistic and missile weapons (`WeaponModes`, pp. 182-192): Ultra ACs fire one or two shots as one attack (2x heat and ammo, cluster column 2, a location per shot); LB 10-X fires slugs or cluster ammo (`ammo:<id>:cluster`, -1 to hit, column 10, 1-point pellets); Streak SRMs hit with every missile, and on a miss don't launch (no heat or ammo); Artemis IV (`<launcher>+artemis`, 1 t, 1 slot) adds +2 to the cluster roll until its slot is hit. - ✅ Rotary autocannons (`RotaryAc`, `rotary_ac2`/`rotary_ac5`, p. 183): 1-6 shots as one attack (heat and ammo per shot, cluster column = shots); more than one shot jams it on an attack roll of 2 (2-3 shots), 3- (4-5) or 4- (6), hit or not; a jammed RAC can't fire until a Gunnery +3 roll at the end of the weapon phase clears it, only if the 'Mech stood still or walked. ## Battle armor (classic record sheets)
Not in the Core Rulebook: general Total Warfare practice, all `TODO(rules)`.
- 🟡 Squads (`BaSpec`, `BaState`, `data/battle_armor/`): 42 official designs (Inner Sphere and Clan, light to assault), MegaMek loadouts, BV from the free sheets (7) or MegaMek's calculator (35; it matches all 7 sheet values), adjusted for skill; per design from MegaMek: mechanized (rides OmniMechs), anti-'Mech (leg and swarm attacks: light or medium, humanoid, not burdened) and magnetic claws (-1 to leg and swarm attacks, +1 to brush or jump them off). Anti-personnel mounts aren't used; support units in the force builder (shared four-unit cap), saved with their troopers.
- 🟡 Fire (`BaFire`): any direction; each weapon is one attack rolled on the Cluster Hits table with a shot (or its missiles) per living trooper; SRM packs have two volleys; +1 to hit battle armor. No heat.
- 🟡 Damage (`BaDamage`): each packet hits one living trooper (1D6, rerolled); damage past its armor kills it and the rest is lost; the squad is destroyed with its last trooper.
- 🟡 Leg attacks (`BaLegAttack`): squads with claws, against an enemy 'Mech in their own hex, as their only attack that turn; to hit = anti-'Mech skill (Piloting) + troopers left (4-6 +0, 3 +2, 2 +5, 1 +7) + target movement, -4 if it can't move; a hit does 4 to a random leg with a through-armor critical chance. The computer climbs legs when it pays. — `TODO(rules)`: attacker movement, prone targets, vibro-claws.
- 🟡 Swarm attacks (`BaSwarm`, `BaBrushOff`): squads with claws, against an enemy 'Mech in their hex, as their only attack; to hit = anti-'Mech skill + 4-6 troopers +2 / 1-3 +5 + target movement, prone -2, immobile -4, plus the Swarm Attack Modifiers table for friendly battle armor riding it; one squad per 'Mech. Once on, it moves with the 'Mech and can't walk, and each later turn its weapons hit automatically, each hit on the Swarm Attacks Hit Location table. The 'Mech can swat it off as its physical attack (each arm, Piloting +4, punch damage to one trooper; a miss punches the 'Mech itself); it drops off a destroyed 'Mech; the squad can let go as its attack. `BaShakeOff`: any fall throws the swarmers off (2D6 to one trooper, drowned in water); dropping prone on purpose takes a piloting roll and, if made, the 'Mech falls and throws them off; landing a jump takes a piloting roll at +4 and, if made, throws them off with 1 damage per jump MP to each trooper (drowned in water). Deep water alone doesn't drown battle armor (only conventional infantry). The computer swarms when it pays and swats swarmers off. — `TODO(rules)`: from MegaMek's Total Warfare (p. 222), not the Core Rulebook; magnetic clamps, swarming vehicles, the computer jumping or dropping to shake swarmers.
- 🟡 Riding OmniMechs (`AssetTransport`, `BaRiders`): a mechanized squad (claws) boards a friendly OmniMech in its hex, one squad per 'Mech; while aboard it isn't a unit and can't be shot. Troopers sit on the Transport Positions spots (1 RT, 2 LT, 3 RT rear, 4 LT rear, 5 CT rear, 6 CT); a hit on a spot with a living trooper hits it first and the rest goes on to the 'Mech. It climbs down into the 'Mech's hex and can't attack that turn; if the 'Mech is destroyed it is set down there. The computer picks squads up when the enemy is far. — `TODO(rules)`: falls with riders, the carrier's own attack limits, damage overflow.
- 🟡 Movement (`BaMoveCost`): ground MP and jump MP with 'Mech terrain costs, no running; no water or buildings (nor landing there); stacks like infantry (`AssetStacking`). No physical attacks or charges by or against them. — `TODO(rules)`: anti-personnel weapons, buildings, conventional infantry.

## Force building and play
- ✅ Two sides, up to 4 'Mechs each (5 for an all-Clan Star), Battle Value budget (2000-8000); deploy on opposite edges.
- ✅ The catalog filters All / Inner Sphere / Clan and shows each OmniMech chassis once with a button cycling its configurations (`DesignFilter`, `ForceCatalog`); with full explanations it says what Clan tech and OmniMechs mean. Mixed Clan and Inner Sphere forces are allowed.
- ✅ Formations (p. 219, a suggestion, not a rule): a side is a lance of up to 4; an all-Clan side may field a Star of 5 (`ForceBuilder.max_units`), shown on the roster — ⬜ Clan honor rules (zellbrigen).
- 🟡 Battle Value (`BattleValue`, `BvText` explains it) — `TODO(rules)`: simplified BV 2 (rear weapons half, 15 per ton of ammo, no CASE).
- ✅ Pilot skill adjusts Battle Value (`BvSkill`, multipliers in `data/bv_skill.json`); the force builder cycles Green / Regular / Veteran / Elite per side.
- ✅ Hot-seat play, or either side played by the computer (`Bot`): it scores every reachable
  end hex by expected damage dealt vs taken plus heat, picks the best target and holds weapons
  to stay cool, punches/kicks when worth the fall risk, and logs why (when explanations are on).
- 🟡 Bot charges and death from above (`BotCharge`): tried at each end position with an enemy straight ahead, valued as expected damage + a bonus for the target's piloting roll − part of its own damage − the fall from a missed DFA, used only when it beats shooting. Pushes (`BotPhysical`) are valued by the chance to knock the target down, a ledge fall, or shoving it off the map; it picks up a club when its guns would do little and an enemy is within 3 hexes. Aimed shots (`BotAim`): at a sitting target, or with a targeting computer, it aims a shot when the chance of hitting times the value of that location (half the hits land there; finishing the head, CT or an XL side torso is worth most) beats an ordinary shot. ⬜ Attacking buildings; smarter focus fire and retreat.
- ✅ Terrain: clear, rough, rubble, light/heavy woods, pavement, water depth 1-2, hills.
- ✅ Maps: Test Valley, River Crossing, Rolling Hills, Dense Forest, City Outskirts.
- ✅ Generated maps (`MapGenerator`): whole 15 x 17 mapsheets (p. 223), 1, 2 (standard) or 4 sheets; themes Grassland, Woodland, Hills, River Valley, Lake Country, Badlands, City; repeatable seeds. No dry step over 2 levels, no buildings on the home-edge rows. Roads cross water on bridges (`GenBridges`); themes can recolour the ground.
- ✅ Tapping an empty hex explains its movement cost, cover and cooling.
- ⬜ Bridges as structures, fire and smoke.


## Battlefield Support Assets (vehicles, infantry; book pp. 140-151)
The Core Rulebook has only these simplified rules for vehicles and infantry; classic record-sheet vehicles are planned as a separate optional mode.
- ✅ Asset cards (`AssetCard`, `data/assets/`): MP and movement type, fixed TMM, range, Skill (improved Skill), damage x groupings, Destroy Check, Threshold, BSP cost; BV = 20 x BSP (p. 157).
- ✅ Damage without hit locations (`AssetDamage`): rear hits double (not infantry), AE doubled vs infantry, BRA/RCA/RFA halve their Weapon Types, Fire Resistant halves heat weapons; heat from any source is 2 damage per point.
- ✅ Destroy Checks (`DestroyCheck`, End Phase): Threshold reached → 2D6 + 1 at 11+ damage and +1 per further 10; survive → Degradation 1 (2 for conventional infantry hit by AI weapons or fire); at 2 or less the next check fails.
- ✅ Infantry inside a building: +1 Check per building class, max 11 (`InfantryCover`) — `TODO(rules)`: enemy infantry in the same building doesn't cancel it yet.
- ✅ Movement (`AssetMove`, `AssetMoveCost`): fixed MP, declared backward movement halves MP and forbids level changes, 2 MP per level (one per hex, two between road hexes), terrain restrictions by movement type, +1 MP staying on pavement, ice/mud/snow/wind costs, no piloting rolls, infantry turn free, jump infantry jump; fire: 4 damage when starting in it, can't stop in it, foot troops can't cross it — `TODO(rules)`: garages, sand, roofs, VTOL flight levels, multi-hex Assets.
- ✅ Stacking (`AssetStacking`, p. 143): vehicles count as 'Mechs; a 'Mech/vehicle hex holds one friendly and one enemy infantry, an infantry-only hex two each; 'Mechs move through Assets; VTOLs through anything (errata).
- ✅ Asset attacks (`AssetFire`): Skill base, no attacker movement modifier, no minimum range, 360-degree arc (No Turret: front only), one target a turn, infantry fire into their own hex; Assets one level high for LOS, never partial cover.
- ✅ Attacking Assets: fixed TMM or the hex's terrain modifier, whichever is higher (VTOLs no terrain); emplacements -4; 'Mechs can't fire into their own hex; physical attacks only punch, kick, club or physical weapon (no push/charge/DFA, none vs VTOLs); Assets make none.
- ✅ APC X / Mechanized (`AssetTransport`): infantry boards or gets out in the transport's hex (Board/Unload button), battle armor takes 2 points, Mechanized battle armor rides OmniMechs as APC2; riders can't be targeted, die with their transport (mechanized battle armor on an OmniMech jumps clear); no fire and TMM 0 the turn they get out — `TODO(rules)`: starting aboard, boarding timed when the infantry acts rather than at the end of the phase, the computer doesn't use transports. The computer picks up infantry when the enemy is more than 8 hexes away and drops them off within 6 (`BotTransport`).
- ✅ Specials wired into existing systems (`AssetSpecials`): AMS (-4 Cluster Hits), ECM (Guardian bubble), PRB (probe range 4), Swarm (+1 grouping, battle armor +2 and may swarm conventional infantry). NARC: a separate Narc beacon shot (3/6/9) that pods a 'Mech, never an Asset — `TODO(rules)`: allowed without a base attack.
- 🟡 Indirect Fire Special (`AssetIndirect`, p. 145): IF X adds X indirect attacks (5 damage, 7/14/21, -2) used in place of regular attacks when no MP was spent; the Asset loses its TMM that turn; a spotter and the usual indirect modifiers apply without line of sight. Assets spot as Running (+2) except infantry, emplacements and SPOTTER (not moved) — `TODO(rules)`: direct use with own line of sight, lost TMM for shots declared before its own.
- 🟡 VTOL flight levels (`VtolFlight`, pp. 142-144): always at least one level above the hex (woods, buildings, hills), 1 MP per level climbed or dropped, LOS from flight level + 1; range ignores level — `TODO(rules)`: VTOLs fly nap-of-the-earth (lowest legal level), no player-chosen altitude yet; bridges.
- ⬜ Specials still to do: Indirect Fire, Artillery, Arrow IV, Narc, TAG, Popup, Zipline, Commander, Crit-Seeker.
- ⬜ Infantry vs buildings (no damage) — `TODO(rules)`: vehicle Asset attacks on buildings are blocked for now.


## Combat vehicles (classic record sheets)
Not in the Core Rulebook: these follow general Total Warfare practice, all `TODO(rules)`.
- 🟡 Record sheets (`VehicleSpec`, `VehicleLoc`, `data/vehicles/`): 22 official designs (18 Inner Sphere, 4 Clan), MegaMek loadouts, sheet BV; structure = tonnage / 10 rounded up. In the force builder as support (shared four-unit cap, sheet BV adjusted for crew skill), deployed on ground their motive can use, saved and restored with their damage.
- 🟡 Damage (`VehicleDamage`, `VehicleHitTable`, `MotiveDamage`, `VehicleCrits`, `VehicleState`): 2D6 hit table by attack direction (3-5 and 9 call for motive rolls, 2 and 12 for a critical check, no turret: the facing side), armor then structure with no transfer; losing a location destroys the vehicle, losing the turret blows it off. Motive damage 2D6 (+1 rear, +2 side, +2 wheeled, +3 hover): minor/moderate/heavy/major. Structure damage or a 2/12 result: 8+ for one roll on the location's critical table (crew, driver, commander, stabilizers, sensors, weapon jam/destroyed, turret lock/blown off, engine, fuel, ammo). Stunned crews lose a turn. `VehicleText` explains each.
- 🟡 Movement (`VehicleMoveCost`): Cruise = walk, Flank = run (x1.5), backward only cruising, no jumping; 1 MP + 1 per level (one level a hex) + terrain: tracked pay the 'Mech costs, wheeled and hover can't enter woods and pay +2 in rough/rubble, only hover crosses water (on the surface); roads and bridge decks base MP; no buildings. Motive damage cuts MP. No piloting rolls, falls or heat. — `TODO(rules)`: driving rolls, skids, ice/mud/snow, buildings.
- 🟡 Firing (`VehicleFire`): front/side/rear weapons fire into their own arc, the turret all round (locked: forward); +1 per sensor hit (4: none), +1 stabilizer hit, +1 commander hit; 'Mech attacker movement modifiers. As targets: normal movement modifiers, one level high, no partial cover, -4 when immobile or the crew is stunned. No physical attacks by vehicles; punches, kicks and swings at them as at a prone 'Mech; no charges or DFA with vehicles yet. Hovercraft on water ride the surface for line of sight and water combat (Assets too).
