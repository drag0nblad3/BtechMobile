# Sixteen provisional component-first sculpts

This draft is stacked on #297. No production model, game data or rules are changed.

- 16 initial independent anatomy designs with 299 subassemblies, 108,801 selected occupied cells. Individual torso, limb, shoulder and head profiles; separate armless walker, gun-platform and humanoid topologies. Shared construction routines are used, not a claim of independent hand sculpting of every detail.
- 6 models required attachment or island repairs. All sixteen selected source assemblies have zero neutral audit faults (one connected component per part, no shared cells, >= one backed two-layer 2x2 joint patch).
- 4 models have 2 manually marked photo comparisons each (Blackjack, Locust, Centurion, Enforcer); 24 contour trials. Scores in summary.json, detailed source polygons and frozen registration in photo_review_first_four.json. Camera and pose fit remains approximate; no comparisons against production models.
- Other 12: reference leads and anatomical targets recorded in initial files. Not a completed photo-driven reconstruction; variant-specific weapon placement, photos and masks need confirmation. Blackjack, Enforcer, Charger and Highlander reference leads differ from the target variant. Some muzzle apertures need explicit certification. No posed mesh or Godot tests.
- Contact sheet is actual selected source-cell raster, quarter view, including the 3 major revisions from previous request. Not an in-engine image. [Contact sheet](contact_sheet.svg).
- Author.js plus repair.js and the parent branch reconstruction lab reproduce initial and repaired geometry. Selected photo trial geometry is in the review JSON; otherwise use repaired JSON. Source pitch/rig not production-ready.

Next steps: 12 remaining photo registrations and major anatomy adjustments; improve Locust leg proportions and joint fits; verify weapons and pose deformation; then finish the originally requested 30-model expansion.
