# Heavy Clan provisional single-view anatomy pass

Three visually inspected miniature photos, manually estimated outlines on 512px displayed previews, one per chassis; frozen camera and pose fit. Two major candidates each. The selected replacements are provisional, not validated photographic likeness improvements: camera RMS is 11–23 px, and no independent second view was registered. Roof missile bank screen-side placement and barrel counts are visual hypotheses requiring exact variant and anatomical side verification.
